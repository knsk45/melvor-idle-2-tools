# Modifiers: planned article

The base reference deliberately has no modifier controls or character calculator. This page is an editorial plan, not a verified equipment guide.

A future article will explain sources of speed, quantity and XP improvements, with build-specific evidence for each claim. Topics include tool intervals, wood damage, mining prospecting and shatter, fishing bait and priority selection, extra rolls and doubling. Additive/multiplicative stacking, caps, rounding and unlock conditions must be checked against the relevant game methods before publication.

Article acceptance requirements:

- Every claim must identify the compatible game version, channel/build, source package or method token, and verification date.
- A modifier must be classified as speed, quantity, XP, success chance, reward selection, or a state/requirement change before discussing stacking.
- The article must separate intrinsic base mechanics from equipment, consumables, player stats, target state, and save-specific progress.
- Unknown interactions remain `null` or are labelled unverified; no first-game mechanics, save observations, or plausible arithmetic may fill the gap.
- Examples may illustrate a verified rule, but must not become new product settings or modified rates in the fixed tables.

Initial article outline:

1. Scope and evidence contract.
2. Timing, progress, success and reward events by skill family.
3. Quantity, doubling and extra-roll semantics.
4. XP and level-dependent effects.
5. Stacking, caps, rounding and precedence — only where directly verified.
6. Open questions and incompatible builds.

Keep example improvements and progression advice separate from the fixed base tables. Do not infer modified rates from a save or import first-game mechanics. No modifiers are currently applied to the reference.
